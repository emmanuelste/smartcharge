## Brand & Style

This design system establishes an authoritative, mission-critical utility aesthetic engineered for East Africa’s rapid transition to electric mobility. Built under the regulatory framework of EWURA (Energy and Water Utilities Regulatory Authority) April 2026 mandates and backed by Reliance Solutions & Technology (T) Ltd., the interface prioritizes infrastructural trustworthiness, absolute clarity in high-glare equatorial sunlight, and institutional rigor.

The visual direction marries **Corporate Modernism** with **Industrial Utility Telemetry**. It avoids ephemeral consumer gradients in favor of dense, high-contrast, structured surfaces that reassure commercial fleet operators, boda-boda charging micro-franchisees, and private EV motorists alike. Visual touchpoints combine civic authority (deep parastatal navy) with decisive kinetic feedback (high-voltage energy green), reinforced by crisp regulatory credentialing, tactile hardware telemetry patterns, and low-latency transactional feedback.

## Colors

The color palette is anchored in utility infrastructure, regulatory compliance, and rapid status discernment:

- **Primary (`#1B365D` - Grid Navy):** The institutional anchor representing grid integrity, EWURA compliance, and corporate reliability. Used for dominant top-level surfaces, persistent structural navigation, regulatory banners, and high-weight text.
- **Secondary (`#00A859` - Energy Kinetic Green):** High-efficiency charge flow indicator. Applied exclusively to active charging session states, positive energy metrics, successful mobile-money reconciliations, and primary action triggers.
- **Tertiary (`#00B4D8` - Kilowatt Cyan):** Represents high-speed DC fast-charging, networked station connectivity, and live telemetry data feeds.
- **Neutral (`#1F242E` - Basalt Slate):** Heavy industrial carbon-neutral for body copy, hardened hardware frame representations, and edge boundaries.
- **Functional Signals:**
  - **Grid Alert Amber (`#F59E0B`):** Off-peak tariff windows, grid throttle notifications, and queuing delays.
  - **Safety Fault Red (`#DC2626`):** Hardware trip, ground fault, emergency stop, and non-compliant station lockouts.
  - **Surface Canvas (`#F8FAFC` to `#FFFFFF`):** High-albedo neutral ground engineered for extreme ambient legibility outdoors.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy implements a dual-purpose system: human-centric institutional clarity and calibrated machine telemetry.

1. **Headlines (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Robust, clear-apertured geometric grotesk that delivers immediate legibility on mobile field screens and roadside digital displays. Headings maintain tight letterforms for spatial density.
2. **Body & Interface (Inter):** Highly legible across varied pixel densities and screen technologies, ensuring crisp rendering of bilingual compliance copy (Swahili and English) and transaction narratives.
3. **Telemetry & Ledger (JetBrains Mono):** Reserved for technical readouts, hardware session counters, kilowatt-hour meters, TZS/kWh currency units, EWURA certification license strings, and receipt hashes. Monospaced alignment prevents tabular jitter during high-frequency live charging updates.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built upon an 8pt architectural rhythm, optimized for rapid operational access and one-handed thumb interaction in field conditions.

- **Grid Strategy:**
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column fluid layout with `margin: 1rem` and `gutter: 1rem`. Bottom-anchored action bars prioritize primary actions like "Initiate Session", "Authenticate NFC", and "Emergency Cut-Off".
  - **Tablet (768px - 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin-tablet: 2rem`, tailored for micro-franchise kiosk touchscreens and station-management terminals.
  - **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout with a fixed max-width container of `1440px` and `margin-desktop: 3rem` for supervisory control and data acquisition (SCADA) fleet dashboards.
- **Rhythm & Structure:** Dense component layouts (`space-sm` to `space-md`) maintain high information throughput, grouping telemetry data clusters and regulatory compliance badges into unified contextual blocks without redundant whitespace.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects exaggerated blur radii in favor of **Crisp Low-Contrast Outlines** combined with **Tonal Infrastructure Layering**:

- **Flat/Resting (`Elevation 0`):** Default card surfaces rest flat on the `#F8FAFC` canvas, bounded by a structural `1px` border in `#E2E8F0` (`#1F242E` at 12% alpha).
- **Surface Elevation (`Elevation 1`):** Applied to active station modules, connector status cards, and payment drawers. Defined by an ambient, sun-resistant drop shadow: `0 2px 4px -1px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.06), 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.08)` with a 1px solid top border highlight.
- **Floating/Modal (`Elevation 2`):** Station fault warnings, QR code validation sheets, and payment processing drawers utilize `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.12), 0 4px 6px -2px rgba(27, 54, 93, 0.04)`.
- **Telemetry Readout Layering:** Dynamic metrics (volts, amps, state of charge) are recessed into pseudo-hardware bezels using an inset border treatment (`inset 0 1px 2px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`) with a cool-tinted dark background (`#0F172A`) to evoke calibrated OLED field monitors.

## Shapes

The design system implements a **Soft Industrial (`roundedness: 1`)** shape language.

- **Base Radius (`0.25rem` / `4px`):** Applied to data chips, regulatory status tags, receipt items, input fields, and telemetry badges.
- **Component Radius (`rounded-lg` / `0.5rem` / `8px`):** Applied to interactive buttons, station selection cards, modal panels, and transaction summaries.
- **Large Surface Radius (`rounded-xl` / `0.75rem` / `12px`):** Dedicated to high-level container views, bottom sheets, and dashboard summary widgets.
- **Strictly Functional Rounding:** Pure pill radii are prohibited for general buttons to preserve the utilitarian tone; full circles are strictly reserved for step-progress rings, socket connector pinouts, and hardware status LEDs.

## Components

### Buttons & Interactive Controls
- **Primary Utility Action:** Solid Grid Navy (`#1B365D`) fill, white text, 8px border radius, 48px standard touch target. Focus states present an outer 2px ring in Energy Green (`#00A859`).
- **Live Action / Start Charge:** Vibrant Energy Green (`#00A859`) fill, bold high-contrast dark navy text (`#0A1D37`).
- **Emergency / Cut-off:** High-contrast Safety Red (`#DC2626`) with solid white uppercase text, bordered by a heavy 2px warning track.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Transparent background with a 1.5px border in `#1B365D`, text colored in `#1B365D`.

### Telemetry Displays & Metric Gauges
- **Live SOC (State of Charge):** Embedded in an OLED-style `#0F172A` capsule. Numerical values rendered in `JetBrains Mono` (`label-lg`) with high-visibility cyan (`#00B4D8`) or green (`#00A859`) text.
- **Session Ticker:** Running session costs (TZS) and cumulative kWh consumption paired with an active pulsing status LED dot (`6px` circular indicator).

### Regulatory Badges & Micro-Franchise Tokens
- **EWURA April 2026 Certification Badge:** Distinctive structural tag featuring a solid `#1B365D` boundary, a faint neutral fill, and the official regulatory identifier in bold monospaced typography (`label-sm`).
- **Operator License Token:** Compact pill badge indicating franchise operator authenticity ("Reliance Verified Operator - Class A"), incorporating a micro lock icon.

### Form Inputs & Mobile-Money Triggers
- **Inputs:** Crisp 1px `#CBD5E1` boundary expanding to 2px `#1B365D` on focus. Swahili/English dual-language placeholder copy.
- **Payment Method Selectors:** Dedicated cards for M-Pesa, Airtel Money, Tigo Pesa, and HaloPesa with integrated USSD fast-handshake feedback badges.

### Connector Cards (CCS2, CHAdeMO, Type 2, GB/T)
- Card frames highlight current state via color-coded left borders (4px wide): Green for Available, Cyan for Charging, Amber for Reserved, Red for Out of Service.
- Incorporates real-time tariff calculations per kWh directly visible prior to selection.
